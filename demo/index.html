      Use your own testnet wallet

      Connect Meteor, Intear or any wallet that lists testnet. The With Screen lane then asks your wallet to sign, and Screen still answers first.

      One option on the connector. Every wallet inherits it.

      Screen sits in NEAR Connect, between your app and the wallet. It reads the receiver and every action in the transaction, so Meteor, Intear, MyNearWallet and any sandboxed, injected or parent-frame wallet are covered by the same check.

      A block throws ScreenBlockedError and the wallet is never contacted. A warn opens a dialog that asks a human before the wallet signs. A clear result passes straight through.

      • OFAC sanctioned addresses1,081 digital-currency addresses from the Treasury SDN list, 137 of them Ethereum-format addresses that NEAR accepts as accounts.

      • New or changed contractsReceivers whose account or code is newer than the freshness window.
      • Dangerous keys and swapsFull-access AddKey, wide function-call keys, and Ref swaps with a far-below-market minimum output.
